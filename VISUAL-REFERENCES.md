# Visual references

The primary references are the user-supplied in-game R.I.P. loading screen and Choose Wisely screen: orange phosphor text, dark monitor glass, gravestone cards, and the physical casing around the display. The supplied Candy Vault wiki PDF provides game context.

Online research was attempted for DOORS Candy Vault R.I.P., Revenant Interface Program, and gameplay footage. The available image results were unrelated to the specific terminal, so they were not used. The wiki terminal page was inaccessible and the YouTube search fetch was throttled. No claim is made that specific online footage was watched or verified.
